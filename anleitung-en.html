1. Overview and first steps

My Whiskey Collection is your personal whiskey collection in your pocket: you manage stock, storage slots, a wishlist and statistics. Everything runs on your device; a sync code lets you connect several devices.

At the bottom (on PC and iPad landscape: on the left) you find the bar with seven areas:

AreaPurpose
CollectionAll whiskeys, search, filters, sorting, add a whiskey, drink
StatisticsEvaluations of stock, prices, storage, drinking history
HistoryThe whiskeys you have drunk
RacksStorage plan: store and move whiskeys, QR labels
WishlistWhiskeys you still want to buy
Export/ImportBack up your data, read in tables and backups
SetupName, currency, language, cloud, define racks

The first five steps

  1. Choose language and currency: Setup → General. The currency follows the language automatically.
  2. Define racks (optional): Setup → Racks (see section 5).
  3. Add your first whiskey: tap + in the collection (section 2).
  4. Store it: give the whiskey a slot in a rack.
  5. Back up: Export/Import → JSON backup, or set up cloud sync (section 11).
The Setup sections are collapsible. “General” is open on first start, the others are closed. The app remembers what you opened or closed last.

2. Adding a whiskey

Tap + in the collection. There are several ways to add a whiskey – they can be combined.

a) By hand

Fill in the fields: Name / Distillery, Year, Bottles, Whiskey type, Region (e.g. Speyside, Islay, Kentucky), Country, Distillery, Age (years), ABV (%), Cask, Storage slot, Serving temperature, Price, Rating, Note. Only the name is required. Save creates the whiskey.

  • Year: The year is the bottling or distillation year of the bottle. Age is the maturation time in years (e.g. 12), ABV the alcohol content in percent and Cask the type of cask (e.g. sherry, bourbon barrel).
  • Stock, Min and Max: If you enter a minimum, the app warns you when the stock falls below it and offers to put the whiskey on the wishlist (section 3).
  • Own photo: “Take / choose photo” attaches a photo to the whiskey. It stays on this device and is included in the JSON backup, but is not synchronised to the cloud.

b) Scan label (AI)

Tap Scan label (AI) and photograph the bottle label. The AI reads name, year, type, region, country, distillery, age, ABV and cask and estimates the serving temperature. Check the values and save. Before the first use the app asks for your consent (section 12).

c) Scan barcode

Tap Scan barcode and hold the bottle's barcode in the frame. The app looks the number up in a free product database (no cost, no AI credit) and, if found, fills in name, producer and whiskey type. It completes the year from the name or asks for it.

  • If the bottle is already in the collection, the app asks “Add another bottle?” – a quick way to count up when you buy more.
  • If the barcode is not in the database (common for smaller distilleries), a hint with the button Scan label (AI) appears. The number is still saved with the whiskey so the bottle is recognised immediately next time.

d) Look up whiskey (AI)

Look up whiskey (AI) lets the AI search the web for region, country, distillery, age, ABV, cask, serving temperature, current average price and – in the web version – a bottle image. Fields you already filled in are not overwritten. This also happens automatically once name and year are entered and there is no price yet.

The iPhone app does not load bottle images from the internet (App Store rule). You can take your own photos instead.

3. The collection

Search, filter, sort

  • Search at the top: finds whiskeys by name, region, country and distillery.
  • Filters: All, Single Malt, Bourbon / Rye, Blended, World, Other. Clicking a type clears the search automatically.
  • Sorting: Newest first, Name, Year, Distillery, Region or Country.
  • List / cards: The List button switches between a compact list and detailed cards.

The whiskey card

The card shows name, year, distillery, rating (stars), region and country (tap to open a map or the country's whisky regions), the number of bottles, the serving temperature (🌡) and the storage slots (tap to jump to the rack). The small tumbler glass symbol opens Wikipedia for the distillery.

At the bottom of the card: Edit, Drink and 🗑 (delete whiskey).

Stock, Min/Max and the shopping list

If you enter a minimum (and optionally a maximum) for a whiskey, the card shows “Stock · Min · Max” and warns with ⚠ when you are below it. The Reorder button opens the shopping list: it lists all whiskeys under their minimum (the target is the maximum, otherwise the minimum). You can share or copy the list, or put the whiskeys on the wishlist.

4. Drinking a whiskey

Tap Drink on the whiskey card. The Drink whiskey dialog opens:

  • Taken from which slot? – choose the rack slot the bottle came from, or “Bottle not stored”. The slot becomes free.
  • Drunk with whom? – names of your guests (optional).
  • Occasion – Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, Birthday, Party or “Just because” (several allowed).

Drink removes one bottle and creates an entry in the History. Confetti and sound celebrate it (can be switched off in Setup). If it is the last bottle, the app tells you: the whiskey then disappears from the collection (but stays in the History).

Whiskey is best served neat at about 16–20 °C; the app shows this estimate as the serving temperature.

Quick by scan: The round 🥃 button in the collection photographs a label, recognises the whiskey in your collection and opens the Drink dialog right away.

5. Racks and storing

Defining and editing racks

Setup → Racks (tap the heading to open): enter name, levels, slots per level and bottles per slot, then tap ＋. “Bottles/slot” is 1 for a bottle rack and larger for a floor or crate. In the list each rack has:

  • the handle ⠿ to change the order, and the Sort A–Z button below the list,
  • ▦ for QR labels, ✏️ to edit, 🧹 to empty and 🗑 to delete.

With edit (✏️) you can rename the rack, change the slots per level, add levels on top or at the bottom, remove levels, and give each level its own name and bottle count. Bottles that lose their slot this way count as “not stored” afterwards; the whiskeys themselves stay in the collection. The app asks before such changes.

The Racks area

At the top you choose a rack with the tiles (name and “occupied / slots”). The order can also be changed here: on a PC drag a tile, on touch screens press and hold a tile for about half a second, then drag. Below is the storage plan: each level with its slots. Occupied slots show a bottle in the colour of the whiskey type.

Storing

  1. Tap a whiskey under “Unassigned” (the list can be filtered by distillery and country) – or tap a “not stored” hint in the collection.
  2. Tap a free slot. For floors and crates the app stores as many bottles as fit.

Scan whiskey & store photographs the label, finds the whiskey and guides you through storing.

Occupied slots, moving, removing

Tap an occupied slot: you see the whiskey and can open it, remove it from the slot or move it (then tap a free slot). Empty rack takes all bottles out of the rack (after confirmation).

QR labels and “Scan level”

With QR labels (Setup → Racks → ▦) you create one label for the rack and one for each level. Share as PDF or print (label sheet 63.5 × 38.1 mm, 21 per page) and stick them on. Then: Scan level in the Racks area – you see what stands on that level and can store here, take out, drink, move a bottle or move the whole level. In the “Add whiskey” form, scanning a level label also fills in the storage slot.

6. History

The History lists all whiskeys you have drunk with date, whiskey, value, guests and occasion. You can filter by whiskey type and occasion and edit an entry (guests, occasion). The History feeds the evaluations “Drunk per month/year”, the spending and the frequency in the Statistics.

7. Wishlist

  • + Add wish: by hand or by label scan, with an optional note (“Where to buy?”).
  • Find prices (💶): The app searches the web for prices of the whiskey, shows the cheapest hit and multiplies by the quantity. These are indicative values without guarantee – please check shipping costs and availability with the shop.
  • Tick on the left: mark as bought/done (the row fades).
  • → Collection: moves the wish into the collection (with quantity and, if available, the cheapest price) and, if you have racks, takes you straight to storing.
  • If a whiskey falls below its minimum, the app offers to put it on the wishlist with the missing quantity (“reorder”).

8. Statistics

From top to bottom:

  • Key figures: bottles, whiskeys, total value, avg rating.
  • By type and Top whiskeys (by rating or frequency, filterable by stars).
  • By country, region, distillery: bars (up to 8, the rest as “+n more”); a tap searches the collection. By year: columns, scrollable sideways.
  • Prices: avg per bottle, cheapest, most expensive, price ranges (matched to your currency) and value by country.
  • Storage: occupancy in percent, free slots, bottles not stored and a bar per rack (a tap jumps to the rack).
  • Drunk: per month (last 12) and per year, plus the spending on bottles drunk.
  • Best countries and best distilleries by average rating (collection and History together).
  • Reorder: whiskeys below minimum stock (opens the shopping list), open wishes and their value.

Sections without data (e.g. without prices) are hidden.

Make a JSON backup regularly. The JSON import replaces the collection with the backup (the app asks first). On a new device this restores everything. Alternatively use cloud sync (section 11).

10. Setup (settings)

General

  • Display name (title): “Peter” becomes “Peter's Whiskey Collection” etc. Leave empty for the default title.
  • Currency: €, $, £, CHF, A$, C$, SEK, NOK, DKK, PLN, HUF, CZK, RON, RUB, UAH, JPY, CNY, KRW, INR.
  • Language: 25 language variants, including German, English, English (US) and many more. The currency changes automatically (Swedish → krona, English (US) → US $, English (UK) → £). Example: when you switch to Swedish the app changes to SEK and asks whether to convert all prices at today's European Central Bank rate (“OK”) or only change the label (“Cancel”). There are no rates for RUB and UAH. You can change the currency by hand at any time.
  • Confetti & sound when drinking: both, confetti only, or off.
  • Font: ten fonts to choose from.
  • Temperature unit: °C or °F (English (US) sets °F automatically).

Storage

This device only or Cloud (sync code, section 11).

AI credits (iPhone app only)

Shows the remaining AI scans, the device ID and – when needed – Add credits or an unlock code. See section 12.

Whiskey database

  • Update all whiskeys (AI): fills in missing region, country, distillery, age, ABV, cask, serving temperature and price for all whiskeys (in the web version also bottle images). Existing values stay. If serving temperatures already exist, the app first asks whether they should also be replaced by AI values. Tip: export a JSON backup first.

Racks

See section 5. The heading shows the number of racks.

11. Syncing two devices

  1. First device: Setup → Storage → Cloud → Create new sync code. The code appears on screen; with Save code via email you send it to yourself.
  2. Second device: Setup → Storage → Cloud → enter the code (lowercase, no spaces) → Connect. If the device already contains whiskeys, the app asks whether to merge them with the cloud data.
  3. After that both devices sync automatically (e.g. when the app is opened); Sync now triggers it by hand. The rule is: the latest change wins.

Whiskeys, wishlist, history, racks and settings such as language and currency are synchronised. Not synchronised are own photos and device-specific data. Anyone who knows your code can see your whiskeys – only share it with people you trust. Also keep a JSON backup.

12. AI, privacy and costs

  • What is AI used for? Scan label, look up whiskey, find prices and “Update all whiskeys”. The photo or the whiskey name is sent via our server to an AI service (Anthropic, USA) for evaluation – without names or accounts. The app asks for your consent before the first use.
  • Barcode scan only sends the number to a free product database and costs no AI credit.
  • Costs (iPhone app): 50 AI calls per installation are included in the purchase price; more can be bought as an optional one-time purchase (no subscription, credits do not expire). Everything else in the app costs nothing extra. When the credits are used up, everything except the AI functions keeps working.
  • Data: Everything stays on your device as long as you do not enable the cloud. No ads, no tracking, no user account. Details in the privacy policy (German).

13. Tips and FAQ

  • On an iPad or PC the layout adapts: wider column, several cards side by side, and on PC (and iPad landscape) the navigation on the left. The phone also works in landscape. On a PC Esc closes the top window; on a phone swipe a window down to close it.
  • My whiskeys are gone: See Support – restore with the sync code or a JSON backup.
  • No barcode hit for a whiskey: Normal for small distilleries. Use the label scan.
  • The AI misread something: Correct the field by hand and save; the AI values are suggestions.
  • Should I let a whiskey “age” in the bottle? No. Whiskey only matures in the cask, not in the bottle – a bottle does not get better with the years, opened or unopened. That is why there is no drinking-window feature; the serving temperature (neat, about 16–20 °C) is just a guideline.
  • The page still shows the old version (web): Reload the page (on PC Ctrl+F5).
